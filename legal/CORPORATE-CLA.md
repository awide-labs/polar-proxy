# Corporate Contributor License Agreement (CCLA)

Version 1.0, effective 2026-07-13

This Corporate Contributor License Agreement ("Agreement") is between the
entity identified below ("Corporation") and Awide Labs Ltd. regarding
Contributions to the Polar Proxy project (the "Project").

Please read this agreement carefully. An authorized representative of the
Corporation must sign on the Corporation's behalf.

## 1. Definitions

"Contribution" and "Project" have the meanings given in
[INDIVIDUAL-CLA.md](./INDIVIDUAL-CLA.md).

"Designated Employees" means employees and contractors of the Corporation
who are identified in writing to Awide Labs Ltd. as authorized to submit
Contributions on behalf of the Corporation.

## 2. Copyright and Patent Grants

The Corporation grants to Awide Labs Ltd. the same copyright and patent
licenses described in Sections 2 and 3 of the Individual Contributor
License Agreement, but only with respect to Contributions intentionally
submitted on behalf of the Corporation by Designated Employees.

## 3. Representations

The Corporation represents that:

1. The signatory below is authorized to bind the Corporation to this
   Agreement.
2. Each Contribution submitted by a Designated Employee is submitted with
   the Corporation's authority.
3. The Corporation will maintain an internal process to ensure
   Contributions comply with the Corporation's intellectual property
   policies.

## 4. Project Licensing and Miscellaneous

Sections 5 through 7 of [INDIVIDUAL-CLA.md](./INDIVIDUAL-CLA.md) apply to
this Agreement, mutatis mutandis.
