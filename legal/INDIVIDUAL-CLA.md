## How to Sign

1. Open a [CLA signing issue](https://github.com/awide-labs/polar-proxy-proxy/issues/new?template=cla_signing.yml)
   or email info@awide.io with subject line `Polar Proxy ICLA`.
2. Wait for a maintainer to confirm your signature on file.
3. When you open a pull request, a maintainer will add the `cla-signed` label
   after verification (required for merge).

Email alternative — include the following statement (fill in your details):

```
I have read and agree to the Individual Contributor License Agreement
(version 1.0) for Polar Proxy:
https://github.com/awide-labs/polar-proxy-proxy/blob/master/legal/INDIVIDUAL-CLA.md

- Full legal name:
- GitHub username:
- Email address:
- Date (YYYY-MM-DD):
```

3. Keep using the same GitHub account for contributions after signing.

For contributions made on behalf of a company, use
[CORPORATE-CLA.md](./CORPORATE-CLA.md) instead.
